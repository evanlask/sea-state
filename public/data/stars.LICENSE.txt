public/data/stars.bin.gz — star catalogue data
==============================================

Contents
  8404 stars with V <= 6.5 from the Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th Revised
  Edition (BSC5): J2000 right ascension and declination, Johnson V magnitude
  and B-V colour index, as little-endian float32 records (format documented in
  src/sim/astro/stars.ts). For the 244 stars without a B-V value, B-V was
  derived from the converter's approximate colour temperature (field "K").

Original catalogue
  Hoffleit, D. & Warren, W. H. Jr. (1991), "The Bright Star Catalogue, 5th
  Revised Ed. (Preliminary Version)", Astronomical Data Center, NSSDC/ADC;
  CDS catalogue V/50. Distributed by the Harvard-Smithsonian Center for
  Astrophysics (http://tdc-www.harvard.edu/catalogs/bsc5.html). US-government
  funded astronomical data, distributed without restriction; generally treated
  as public domain.
