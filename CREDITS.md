# Credits

Sea~State is built on open-source code and freely licensed assets. CC0 assets need no credit, but we credit them anyway.

## Built with

- [three.js](https://threejs.org) (MIT): WebGPU renderer and TSL shading language
- Vite+ (MIT): Build, test and lint toolchain

## Algorithms

- Jerry Tessendorf: Simulating Ocean Water — FFT ocean synthesis
- Christopher J. Horvath: Empirical directional wave spectra for computer graphics
- Sébastien Hillaire: A scalable and production-ready sky and atmosphere rendering technique
- Andrew Schneider (Guerrilla): Nubis — real-time volumetric cloudscapes
- Jacques Kerner: Water interaction model for boats in video games
- Miles Macklin et al.: XPBD — position-based simulation of compliant constraints
- J. Holtrop & G. G. J. Mennen: An approximate power prediction method
- Werner Blendermann: Wind loading of ships — windage coefficients
- H. Yasukawa & Y. Yoshimura: MMG standard method for ship manoeuvring predictions

## Data

- [Yale Bright Star Catalogue](https://github.com/brettonw/YaleBrightStarCatalog) (MIT): via brettonw/YaleBrightStarCatalog
- [ORC sailboat data](https://github.com/jieter/orc-data): Velocity polars via jieter/orc-data
- SIMMAN KCS benchmark: Container ship hull and manoeuvring data
